## Packing Barn: finish the inside job before closing the outside job

The barn has 3 baseline crates with 6 fruit in each. The important boundary is when sealing happens: only after every fruit in the current crate has been inspected and resolved.

## Put seal after the inner loop

In ``||farm(noclick):Farm jobs||``, find ``||farm(noclick):on packing requested||``, ``||farm(noclick):crates to pack for job job||``, ``||farm(noclick):fruit in each crate for job job||``, ``||farm(noclick):open next crate for job job||``, ``||farm(noclick):inspect next fruit for job job||``, ``||farm(noclick):current fruit damaged for job job||``, ``||farm(noclick):compost current fruit for job job||``, ``||farm(noclick):pack current fruit for job job||``, and ``||farm(noclick):seal current crate for job job||``.

Use the outer loop for crates. Open one crate, then use the inner loop for that crate's fruit. Inspect each fruit before deciding: damaged fruit goes to compost; otherwise pack it.

After the inner fruit loop has finished, seal the current crate. The seal block is still inside the outer loop, but it is **after** the inner loop.

Run the Packing Barn. If sealing is accidentally inside the fruit loop, the crate should visibly refuse/bounce because unresolved fruit remains.

## Retrieve the resource while pattern for Lanternleaf

At Defense Prep, Lanternleaf reuses the resource-controlled while pattern. In ``||defenders(noclick):Defender powers||``, find ``||defenders(noclick):on Lanternleaf power requested||``, ``||defenders(noclick):magic charge available at start for job job||``, and ``||defenders(noclick):give next compatible ally 1 energy for job job||``.

Make a learner variable named `charge` and copy the starting charge into it once. While `charge > 0`, reduce `charge` by `1`, then transfer one unit of energy.

Baseline capacity is 6 and the optional capacity upgrade is 8, but actual starting charge may be lower after drain. The same algorithm must respect the reporter's actual starting value.
