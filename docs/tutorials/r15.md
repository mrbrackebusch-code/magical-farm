## Moon Grove: put the pieces together

Moon Grove uses structures you already know rather than introducing a new one: an outer bed loop, a live pest loop, a two-way choice, and a harvest that belongs after the pest loop finishes.

## Complete one bed before moving on

In ``||farm(noclick):Farm jobs||``, find ``||farm(noclick):on moon grove work requested||``, ``||farm(noclick):moon grove beds for job job||``, ``||farm(noclick):move to next bed for job job||``, ``||farm(noclick):current bed has pests for job job||``, ``||farm(noclick):current crop threatened for job job||``, ``||farm(noclick):protect current bed for job job||``, ``||farm(noclick):tend current bed for job job||``, and ``||farm(noclick):harvest current bed for job job||``.

Treat one outer-loop iteration as one complete bed. Move to the bed, then stay in a `while` loop while that bed still has pests.

Inside the while loop, use `if/else`: if the current crop is threatened, protect it; otherwise tend it.

Harvest only after the pest loop has ended for that bed. Then the outer loop can advance to the next bed.

## Let Rootsnare's live world state stop the loop

At Defense Prep, Rootsnare uses a `while` loop with **no learner counter at all**. In ``||defenders(noclick):Defender powers||``, find ``||defenders(noclick):on Rootsnare power requested||``, ``||defenders(noclick):root patch reach for job job||``, ``||defenders(noclick):enemy trapped within reach tiles for job job||``, ``||defenders(noclick):tighten roots for job job||``, and ``||defenders(noclick):slow trapped enemy for job job||``.

Use **enemy trapped** as the live while condition, and plug **root patch reach for job job** into its reach input. Inside the loop, tighten roots and then slow the trapped enemy.

The baseline patch reach is 1 tile; the optional upgrade makes it 2. The world can end the loop when the target dies, escapes, phases, or otherwise stops being a valid trapped target.
