Art assets were generously taken from [Isaiah658 on OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org/users/isaiah658).

His supplied Pixel Packs and monster pack are CC0. Credit is given with thanks.

The game uses thirteen selected designs from his **50+ Monsters Pack 2D**,
with deliberately mapped shared-palette colors and unchanged 64-pixel shapes.
Front/back views, a small hop and blinking provide their game animation.

Magical plant images are accepted project prototypes. Their mature pixels are
preserved; growth cues are smaller versions of the same art. Editable source
images and color mappings are retained in the authoring project.

Uses the "16x16 RPG Tileset" by hilau at https://opengameart.org/content/16x16-rpg-tileset, which is based off of "16x16 Game Assets" by George Bailey at https://opengameart.org/content/16x16-game-assets. and "LPC Thatched-roof Cottage" by bluecarrot16 at https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-thatched-roof-cottage.

The cottage also credits [Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm) and Daniel Armstrong
(HughSpectrum), LPC Base Assets](https://opengameart.org/content/liberated-pixel-cup-lpc-base-assets-sprites-map-tiles),
and [Casper Nilsson, LPC art entry](https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-cnilsson).
